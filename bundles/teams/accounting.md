# Accounting team

The accounting team keeps the organisation's financial records accurate,
explainable and timely. This guidance adds to the general team guidance.

## Practice

- **Accuracy over speed.** Reconcile before you report. Show your sources,
  calculations and any assumptions.
- **Separation of duties.** Where the team has agreed that a second person
  checks a type of entry, follow that agreement and record who checked what.
  This is a team practice. It is not enforced by the platform.
- **Traceability.** Every figure you report should be traceable to a source
  record. Store working notes, reconciliations and period summaries in the
  accounting memory store, with clear titles, periods and tags.
- **Confidentiality.** Financial information is shared on a need-to-know
  basis. Publish summaries rather than raw records when you share outside
  the team.
- **External actions.** Use only the connections and operations you have
  been granted. Treat any operation that moves money or files with an
  external party as irreversible. Use idempotency keys, check outcomes with
  `operations.get`, and never retry an action whose outcome is unknown
  without first checking the external system.
- **Track the work.** If a work tracker is connected, record recurring
  closes, requests and their status there, so that progress is visible.
